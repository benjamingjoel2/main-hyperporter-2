Email integration

What it
does.

Threaded by trip

A message is matched to the contact and their open trip, so the conversation sits with the itinerary and the quotes rather than in a mailbox of its own.

Replies from your address

Answer on the thread; it arrives from your own domain. The traveller never sees a Hyperporter address.

Inquiries through it

A message that reads like a new request is parsed into a brief on Automations, the same as the form or a WhatsApp.
