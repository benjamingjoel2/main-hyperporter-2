Core features

Six things
from your data.

  • 01

    Vault

    Contracts and rates, securely held and queryable, the foundation everything else reads from.

  • 02

    Memory

    Carries forward your quoting style, preferred suppliers, and traveller preferences, so Atlas isn't re-taught from scratch on every trip.

  • 03

    AI chat over your own data

    Ask questions directly against your own contracts and rates in Vault. Ask it to show billing, open a ticket or start a new inquiry, and it takes you there.

  • 04

    AI-assisted quoting

    Drafts a quote from your real contracted rates, not a generic estimate.

  • 05

    AI itinerary suggestions

    Proposes day-by-day structure from your own suppliers and past itineraries, and Memory of what's worked before.

  • 06

    Yours first

    Your own Vault reads from your own data before anything else does. Atlas is trained on it, not on anyone else's.

How it processes

Queryable in minutes
not weeks.

Drop in years of contracts, rate sheets, and past itineraries. Atlas reads, structures, and indexes all of it into Vault, ready to query the same day, not after a manual data-entry project.

Two segments

One layer
either side.

Travel agency: quoting from your own negotiated rates, not a guess.

An agency with years of DMC contracts stops re-reading PDFs for every quote. Atlas drafts from the actual contracted rate, remembers the agency's preferred suppliers, and proposes itineraries structured like the ones that already worked.

Supplier / DMC: AI-assisted quoting, from rates only you have.

A DMC's own rate sheets and seasonal pricing become the source Atlas quotes from, not a generic estimate, and not visible to any other DMC on the network.
