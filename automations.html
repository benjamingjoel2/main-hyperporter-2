01 · Quoting

Automated
Inquiry

A form, an email or a WhatsApp arrives. Automated Inquiry reads it, asks the traveller the questions it cannot answer, and confirms the brief with them before the trip moves on to quotes.

  • Parsing Dates, destination, travellers and budget pulled out of whatever was written, however it was written.
  • The follow-up questions What is missing is asked for, on the channel the message came from, in a short guided exchange.
  • Confirmation The traveller is shown the brief and asked whether it is right. Nothing moves forward until they say so.

02 · Quoting

Automated
Quotation

Each part of the trip (hotels, transport, activities) goes to the suppliers qualified for it, in parallel. Replies come back onto the Quotation Board, your margin is applied, and the traveller never sees a number before you do.

  • Sending One request per part, to each qualified supplier on your list, or a Geotrax supplier where you have none, from the trip's magic link.
  • Chasing A supplier who has not replied by the deadline is reminded. One who declines is replaced with the next qualified one.
  • Collecting Replies land on the board as they arrive, line by line, ready to compare. Your margin is applied on top of each.

Where a person steps in. Choosing which quote to accept is yours. The supplier's quoted rate is what the supplier receives; your margin is added on top and never taken from it.

03 · Quoting

Automated
Confirmation

The moment a proposal is accepted, every supplier on it is asked to re-confirm dates, availability and price. A small change is resolved with the traveller directly. A real problem finds a backup. A person steps in only if neither works.

  • Re-verification Each supplier is asked to confirm the exact dates and rate they quoted, now that the traveller has said yes.
  • Small changes A room category swap or a different transfer time is put to the traveller for a yes, and applied when they give it.
  • Backups A supplier who can no longer deliver is replaced with the next qualified quote from the board, and the traveller is told.

Where a person steps in. If a supplier cannot deliver and there is no qualified backup on the board, it reaches you with the options, not as a silent failure.

04 · Booking

Automated
Collection

The last mile chased down before it is a problem. Passport details from the traveller. Vouchers, pickup times and a driver's number from each supplier. Automated Collection asks for each, tracks what has come in, and reminds whoever is late, so that on departure day nothing is missing.

  • Traveller documents Passport details, dietary needs, arrival flight, requested on the traveller's link and stored on the trip.
  • Supplier confirmations Booking references, vouchers and ground contacts requested from each supplier on their side of the link.
  • A checklist that chases The trip shows what is in and what is outstanding. Reminders escalate as departure gets closer.

05 · Booking

Automated
Conversion

A supplier sends back a booking confirmation on their letterhead, or a voucher with their logo on it. Automated Conversion reads it, takes the details that matter, and issues the same thing as your own document: your name on it, your layout, ready for the traveller.

  • Reading the supplier's document A confirmation or voucher that arrives by email or on the supplier's side of the link is read for the details that matter: reference, dates, times, inclusions, the ground contact.
  • Reissuing it as yours The same booking comes back out on your letterhead, in your layout, under your name. The supplier's branding does not reach the traveller.
  • Filed against the trip Both versions are kept: the supplier's original for your record, and the branded one the traveller receives.

Where a person steps in. A document that cannot be read cleanly (a scan, a handwritten amendment, an inclusion that does not match what was quoted) is flagged to you with both versions side by side rather than reissued on a guess.

06 · Booking

Automated
Payment

The deposit request goes out when the trip is confirmed. The balance request goes out when it falls due. Reminders follow. What never happens on its own is marking money received. That stays a deliberate action, every time.

  • The deposit request Sent to the traveller's link the moment the trip is confirmed, with the amount, the due date and your payment instructions.
  • The balance request Sent on the date you set, with reminders before and after it if unpaid.
  • The supplier's side Once you mark the deposit received, each supplier is told their line is secured. On Enterprise, they are paid their full quoted rate automatically.

Where a person steps in. Marking money received is always yours. Nothing about payment is confirmed silently.

07 · Traveling

Automated Ground
Support

Once the trip is under way, drivers, guides and lodges are briefed from the same record the traveller sees. Late flights, changed pickups and closed roads reach everyone who needs to know, and anything that is not routine reaches a person.

  • Briefed from the trip Each driver, guide and lodge gets the day's plan on their phone: who, where, when and what has been confirmed.
  • Changes passed on A late flight or a changed pickup is sent to everyone it affects, and the traveller is told what happened.
  • Problems raised A closed road, a missing driver or a sick traveller is flagged to a person at once, with the trip and the contacts alongside.

Where a person steps in. Anything that is not routine is flagged straight away. Nothing is rebooked or refunded on its own.

08 · Any stage

Automated
Follow-up

A proposal opened twice and not answered. A voucher still missing five days out. A traveller home for a week with no feedback asked for. Automated Follow-up sends the message you would have sent, at the moment you would have sent it.

  • Proposal nudges A traveller who has opened the proposal and gone quiet gets a short check-in, timed from when they last looked.
  • Pre-departure reminders Vouchers, pickup details and ground contacts are chased from suppliers as departure gets close, with reminders escalating.
  • After the trip A thank-you, a request for feedback and a review ask go out on schedule. The thread stays open until anything raised is resolved.
